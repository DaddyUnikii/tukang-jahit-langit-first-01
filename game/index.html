THE TAILOR'S NOTES

A needle. A little light.

  1. Sew: click two numbered pins, or drag. Thread costs distance. Storms block bridges.
  2. Single thread holds one firefly. Double thread holds three. Rescues make your flock heavier.
  3. Start walking, then Guide: click an adjacent island. Rescue every firefly before reaching the house.
  4. At an island you can Sew again, or Cut a bridge to refund all of its thread. Cut behind you to reach further.
  5. Flowers are optional. Earn one star for finishing, one for all flowers, one for meeting the move target.
